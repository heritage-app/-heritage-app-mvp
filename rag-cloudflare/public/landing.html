Ask

Ask in English. Get it in Ga.

Nii Obodai answers from the Ga archive and marks what people have verified. When it doesn't know, it says so instead of guessing. Rate each answer, and tap thumbs-down to correct one. As you type, it finishes the Ga word for you, ɛ, ɔ and ŋ included: tap space to accept.

Install the app

SpeakGa on your home screen.

Free, no app store. It opens full-screen, loads fast on a weak connection, and keeps working where the signal is poor.

  1. iPhone / iPad
    1. 1Open app.speakga.org in Safari
    2. 2Tap Share
    3. 3Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add
  2. Android
    1. 1Open app.speakga.org in Chrome
    2. 2Tap Install SpeakGa when it appears, or ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen
    3. 3Tap Install
  3. Laptop / desktop
    1. 1Open app.speakga.org in Chrome or Edge
    2. 2Click the install icon at the right of the address bar
    3. 3Click Install. It opens in its own window

On a laptop? Point your phone's camera here to open SpeakGa, then add it to your home screen.

Support the research

Help give Ga a place in the digital future.

SpeakGa is a research project by Codetrain Foundation. The app works today, but Ga still has very little digital language data. Funding and data partners decide how fast that changes.
