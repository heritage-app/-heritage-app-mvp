Ask

Ask in English. Get it in Ga.

Nii Obodai answers from the Ga archive and marks what people have verified. When it doesn't know, it says so instead of guessing. Rate each answer, and tap thumbs-down to correct one. As you type, it suggests the right Ga spelling, ɛ, ɔ and ŋ included.
